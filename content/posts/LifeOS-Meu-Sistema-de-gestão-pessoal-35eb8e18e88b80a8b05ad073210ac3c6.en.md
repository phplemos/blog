# Context

Throughout my life, I always struggled with organization. I was the type of person who felt too lazy to plan because improvisation seemed to get the job done. But as a consequence of living on improvisation, my daily life was chaotic: arriving late to commitments, inconsistent sleep habits, neglecting physical exercise, and constantly reacting rather than anticipating.

Eventually, I woke up to the reality that this wasn't normal, even if it had become *my* normal. I decided to take control of one area of my life at a time and fix it. That moment of waking up and committing to change is what my friend Marcelo Santiago described to me as an **inflection point** — which, in my case, began around May 2023. I decided to pick specific pillars of my life to tidy up, gradually building momentum to tackle the rest.

At the end of the first half of 2023, thanks to friends I'm very grateful for, I joined the gym — a simple habit that I've maintained to this day. I consider that my first real step toward where I am now. In this post, I want to reflect on that journey and compare where I was then to where I am now, exploring the tangible impact of a personal methodology grounded in consistency, focus techniques, and systems thinking.

# My Goal With This Article

My goal with this article is to share the process of bringing structure to my life, documenting the contrast between who I was and who I am at the time of writing this post, and proving to myself that the philosophy of **maintaining consistency** yields exponential compound results. 

If anyone reading this is going through something similar, you might find inspiration in this experiential account — adapting and tuning these ideas to your own reality. Feel free to reach out and share your own experiences with me.
