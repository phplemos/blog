### UniEspaços: From Classroom to Real-World Production

Among my personal projects, the one that brings me the greatest satisfaction is **UniEspaços**. It brings me genuine joy knowing that an initiative that originated inside a Software Engineering I classroom is now running in production at **UESB** (State University of Southwestern Bahia) in Jequié/BA, and is actively expanding across all three university campuses.

This was a project built with 100% dedication, aimed at delivering something genuinely useful for students and faculty. The university embraced the idea and officially deployed it. It has been amazing to see the positive impact across departments: currently serving ~600 active users with an expansion roadmap targeting 5,000 to 10,000 users — presenting me with real-world software engineering challenges at scale.
