## 🏆 Project 1: UniEspaços

**UniEspaços** is the landmark project of my software journey. It began as a practical assignment in Software Engineering I and evolved into my undergraduate capstone thesis. The State University of Southwestern Bahia (**UESB**) recognized the impact of the solution and officially adopted it to govern the reservation and scheduling of all institutional spaces (classrooms, research labs, auditoriums, sports complexes).

Today, it is actively running in production at the Jequié/BA campus and is expanding across all three university campuses (Vitória da Conquista and Itapetinga). It provides an extraordinary engineering experience: currently serving 600+ active users with an expansion roadmap targeting 5,000 to 10,000 users.

The project is 100% open-source and welcoming community contributions!

- 🔗 **Source Code:** [github.com/uniespacos/app](https://github.com/uniespacos/app)
- 📜 **INPI Software Patent:** Granted (BR51202500645-1)
- 🛠️ **Technologies:**
  - **Backend:** PHP 8.2 / Laravel
  - **Frontend:** React, Inertia.js, Tailwind CSS
  - **Database:** PostgreSQL
  - **DevOps & Infra:** Docker, GHCR (GitHub Container Registry), GitHub Actions (CI/CD)

## 🔬 Project 2: ReserveLMP

**ReserveLMP** was a major turning point in my mastery of DevOps, delivery pipelines, and production infrastructure stability. Engineered for UESB's Dean of Research and Graduate Studies, the platform coordinates booking, access control, and usage reporting for high-precision scientific research equipment and multi-user laboratories.

Beyond its technical architecture, the system served a pivotal strategic purpose: it enabled the university to meet rigorous technical compliance standards in federal research grants, helping secure multi-million-reais funding announcements for the institution.

- 📜 **INPI Software Patent:** Granted (BR512025005138-7)
- 🛠️ **Technologies:**
  - **Backend:** PHP / Laravel
  - **Frontend:** React, Inertia.js
  - **Database:** PostgreSQL
  - **DevOps & Infra:** Docker, isolated networks, automated staging and production environments

## ⚡ Project 3: LifeOS

**LifeOS** is my personal framework for life management, goal tracking, habits, and productivity. It was built around the philosophy of applying software engineering principles (such as modularity, fast feedback loops, and iterative refinement) to daily personal routines.

Integrated with the Notion API (and serving as the CMS for this very blog via custom automated synchronization pipelines), LifeOS enables me to define high-level life goals, break them into actionable sprints, and maintain the consistency required to study continuously and share knowledge publicly.

- 🛠️ **Concepts & Tools:**
  - Notion API & headless integrations
  - TypeScript & Node.js for automated sync
  - Time management, personal retrospectives, and technical reading telemetry
